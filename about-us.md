# About REAN

REAN (រៀន means "to learn" in Khmer) is a free educational platform for Cambodia. It
connects students with schools, tutors, and AI-driven learning missions, wrapped in a
gamified community and a points-based economy.

> **Incubated by [CamboVerse Center](https://camboverse.world/), [National University of Management (NUM)](https://numuniversity.com/).**
>
> 🌐 Live at **[rean.camboverse.world](https://rean.camboverse.world)**

## 🎯 Our Mission

REAN exists so that any learner in Cambodia can study for free. Students learn by doing.
They work through project-based missions, get taught and graded by an AI tutor in Khmer
or English, and help each other in a community where answering questions earns
recognition.

The platform is free for everyone in Cambodia to use. The project sustains itself
through community support, donations, grants, and training rather than by selling the
software.

## ✨ What REAN Offers

| Feature                       | What it does                                                        |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Missions**                  | Project-based courses with modules, squads, classes, and AI grading |
| **Schools**                   | School profiles, admissions, enrollments, inquiries, scholarships   |
| **Tutors**                    | Tutor profiles, bookings, and student tutor requests                |
| **Short Courses**             | A lighter course marketplace alongside Missions                     |
| **Kru Rean (AI Tutor)**       | A Gemini-powered chat tutor plus a live voice tutor                 |
| **Community (Lazy Learning)** | A Q&A feed with reactions, accepted answers, and bounties           |
| **Rewards**                   | Leaderboards, achievements, mystery boxes, redeemable rewards       |
| **Roles & Auth**              | Google sign-in with role selection and role-gated dashboards        |

Built with React 18, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and Vite on the front end, Supabase
(PostgreSQL, Auth, Storage, Edge Functions) on the back end, Google Gemini for AI, and
Cloudflare Pages for hosting. See the [README](./README.MD) for the full technical
overview.
